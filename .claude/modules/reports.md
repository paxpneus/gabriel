# Reports module

- `sales-report.repository.ts` — raw SQL building the daily sales-report snapshot. Per the snapshot-is-source-of-truth pattern, values are computed once in `upsertSnapshots` and read from the snapshot table by everyone else. `gtin` column reads `pc.gtin` directly (no `gtin_package` fallback, since `m265` — see `.claude/entities/product/index.md`).
- `SalesReportQueue.applySupplierDiscounts` writes `SalesOrderItemSnapshot.supplier_discount_value`/`.supplier_discount_rule_id` hourly — see `.claude/entities/invoice/supplier-discount/report-value.md` for how the Invoices module reads this.
- See `.claude/entities/order/index.md` for the `orders.internal_status` vs. `sales_order_snapshots.status_snapshot` divergence — `status_snapshot` is computed in this repository's `upsertSnapshots`.
- `product-stock-flow/` — `GET /api/product-stock-flow?start_date&end_date` (YYYY-MM-DD, obrigatórios), permissão `financial-pdt` (`CUSTOM_ROUTE_MAP`). Loja 21 fixa (`CD21_UNIT_BUSINESS_NUMBER`). Entradas = `PURCHASE_ENTRY` (`SUM(net_total_amount)`); saídas = `SALE_OUT` + `MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT` OUT com `invoice_number` (`SUM(unit_price_invoice * qty)`, sem preço não entra no valor e conta em `warnings.outputs_without_price`; entrada sem `net_total_amount` conta em `warnings.inputs_without_net_amount`). Meses em `America/Sao_Paulo`, `months` desc. Repository: 2 agregações (mês×produto); service agrega/consolida em memória (BigInt) e busca nome/sku via `productService`/`productConfigService`. Query objects: genéricos/compartilhados em `src/shared/query/{sequelize-helpers,query-objects/stock-movements}`, específicos em `product-stock-flow/repositories/query-objects/`. `.routes.ts` fica na raiz da pasta do relatório (o loader de `config/routes.ts` para na 1ª pasta com `*.routes.ts`). Ver `.claude/entities/stock-movement.md` pros valores da nota.
